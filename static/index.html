💡 How Single-Channel Selection Works:
  • Only 1 Active Channel at a time: The system automatically pairs the channel's client_secret.json and token.json together.
  • Switching Channels: Click "⚡ Switch to this Channel". If the channel is already authorized, it connects instantly with zero extra setup.
  • First-Time Setup for a new channel: Click Switch to that channel, then click "🔑 Sign In with Google" once. Its token is permanently saved for that channel.
